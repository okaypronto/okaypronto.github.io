    About

    Joseph
    “Pronto”
    Figueroa

    Also known as Okay Pronto (formerly Prince Pronto). Soca producer, songwriter, and sound engineer — from Trinidad, based in New York City.

    Trinidad-born, raised in New Grant, South Trinidad, and now based in New York City. Pronto built a career across the full record — writing, producing, performing, mixing, and mastering. In 2021 he founded Trypp Records, an independent label whose riddim business now feeds Carnival seasons every year.

    His breakout credit: writing and producing the 2025 “Eskimo (Remix)” by Trinidad Killa ft. Nicki Minaj — winner of the Caribbean Music Award for Soca Collaboration of the Year, plus iTunes #2 and Apple Music #50. Before that he wrote “Soca Kingdom” (Machel Montano x Superblue), the Trinidad Road March 2018 winner.

    In October 2026 he became a member of the Recording Academy (GRAMMY U), New York Chapter. The catalog on this site holds 219 records he wrote, produced, performed, mixed, or mastered — every credit backed by its sources.

    Short Bio

    Okay Pronto is a soca producer, songwriter, and sound engineer from Trinidad and Tobago, based in New York City, known for the 2025 breakout “Eskimo (Remix)” by Trinidad Killa featuring Nicki Minaj. Born Joseph Michael Figueroa (December 7, 1997), he founded Trypp Records in 2021. “Eskimo (Remix)”, released February 2025 on Trypp Records, charted iTunes #2 and Apple Music #50, drew 169K+ YouTube views on the official audio, and won the Caribbean Music Award for Soca Collaboration of the Year. A Billboard-charting producer, Okay Pronto has built a catalog of 217 songs spanning production, songwriting, mixing, and mastering, alongside riddim projects.

    Beyond music · Est. 2021

    The Label

    Trypp
    Records

    The independent label Pronto founded in 2021 — run out of New York City, rooted in Trinidad & Tobago. The home of his records, and the engine behind them.

    The riddim business is the backbone. Every Carnival season brings new riddim packs from the label, with voicings from artists across the soca world landing as the season heats up.

    The label’s 2025 breakout was “Eskimo (Remix)” — Trinidad Killa ft. Nicki Minaj — which Pronto wrote, produced, mixed and mastered, and which went on to win the Caribbean Music Award for Soca Collaboration of the Year. “Private Party (The Origin Project)” (Machel Montano, 2021) and the Yahweh Riddim’s “YAHWEH” have followed it into the hits column.

    Beyond his own catalog, Trypp is where artists come to record, mix, master, and produce with him — all six services bookable straight from the Services page.
